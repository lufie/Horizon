## [Discovery Loop: Automating the Scientific and Machine Learning Experimental Loop](https://www.discoveryloop.com/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Discovery Loop is a new venture launched to automate the experimental loop in science and engineering, starting with machine learning research and large-scale systems. The initiative aims to apply this automated approach to address complex subproblems across various scientific domains, including the NAE Grand Challenge problems. Automating the research and experimentation loop could drastically accelerate scientific discovery and machine learning optimization by removing human bottlenecks in trial-and-error processes. This represents a shift toward agentic AI systems capable of autonomously conducting, analyzing, and iterating on complex experiments. The venture requires deep expertise in both machine learning and large-scale systems to build infrastructure capable of running continuous, automated experiments. While starting with software-based ML research, the long-term vision includes scaling these automated loops to broader physical and engineering sciences.

hackernews · xtreak29 · Aug 5, 16:19 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184960)

**Background**: The concept of automated scientific discovery has gained traction with projects like Andrej Karpathy's "autoresearch," which uses AI agents to autonomously run machine learning training and optimization loops. In broader scientific fields, closed-loop automation combines machine learning with robotics or simulation to design, execute, and analyze experiments without human intervention.
