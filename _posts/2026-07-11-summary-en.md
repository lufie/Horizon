## [The Battle Against Aggressive Web Scrapers Using Residential Proxies](https://lwn.net/SubscriberLink/1080822/990a8a5e2d379085/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

LWN published an update detailing the ongoing challenges of defending against aggressive web scrapers, which increasingly route their traffic through residential proxies to bypass traditional IP-based blocking. The update highlights that mitigation techniques like Proof-of-Work (PoW) challenges are proving insufficient against these sophisticated scraping networks. As AI companies and data brokers aggressively scrape the web, traditional defense mechanisms are failing, threatening the availability of independent websites. If left unresolved, this arms race could force websites to adopt intrusive anti-bot walls, ultimately damaging the open web and locking out legitimate users. Residential proxy networks leverage millions of compromised or incentivized consumer devices, making their traffic indistinguishable from legitimate users. Consequently, Proof-of-Work (PoW) defenses like Anubis fail because scrapers can easily offload the computational puzzle solving to this massive pool of hijacked residential machines.

hackernews · chmaynard · Jul 10, 19:38 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48864252)

**Background**: Residential proxies route internet traffic through real home internet connections assigned by ISPs, making them highly trusted compared to datacenter IPs. Proof-of-Work (PoW) bot mitigation is a defense mechanism that requires a client's browser to solve a cryptographic puzzle before accessing a site, aiming to make automated scraping economically and computationally expensive.
