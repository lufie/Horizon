## [Thinking Machines Releases Inkling, an Open-Weights Multimodal Model with Native Audio](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/introducing-inkling/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Thinking Machines has launched Inkling, a new open-weights multimodal model featuring native audio capabilities designed for efficient customization and local deployment. It is highlighted as the largest open-weight model supporting audio, offering a strong foundation for long-context multimodal tasks. Inkling provides developers and enterprises with a highly customizable, cost-effective base model that can be fine-tuned locally or via platforms like Tinker, reducing reliance on closed-source frontier APIs. Its native audio and multimodal capabilities make it highly suitable for next-generation agentic applications and specialized enterprise workflows. While not the absolute strongest model overall, Inkling excels as a base for fine-tuning due to its efficient reasoning and compatibility with local deployment tools like llama.cpp and Unsloth (supporting GGUF and NVFP4 formats). It stands out for its strong long-context window and native integration of audio alongside other modalities.

**Background**: Open-weights models provide public access to their trained parameters, allowing developers to run them locally, customize them for specific tasks, and maintain complete data privacy. Multimodal AI has evolved beyond text and images to natively process audio and video, which is crucial for building more natural and versatile AI assistants.
