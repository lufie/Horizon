## [Terry Tao Shares Insights on Building Math Apps with AI Coding Agents](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/07/11/old-and-new-apps-via-modern-coding-agents/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Fields Medalist Terry Tao published a blog post detailing his hands-on experiences and reflections on using modern AI coding agents to build and update interactive mathematical visualizations and applications. He highlighted how these guided LLM tools allow him to quickly generate non-mission-critical visual supplements for his academic work. This highlights how AI coding assistants are democratizing software development, enabling domain experts outside of traditional computer science to rapidly prototype and create custom tools. It showcases the practical utility of LLMs in academic research and education, unlocking a massive latent demand for specialized software. Tao noted that while LLM-generated code is not always perfect or fully trustworthy, the downside risk is acceptable for generating interactive supplements that are not mission-critical to a paper's core. The workflow relies on guided interaction with AI agents to iteratively refine, debug, and update the applications.

hackernews · subset · Jul 12, 11:09 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48880170)

**Background**: AI coding agents, such as Devin, SWE-Agent, and Cursor, are software systems designed to autonomously or semi-autonomously execute tasks across the software development lifecycle. These agents leverage large language models (LLMs) to write, test, and debug code based on natural language prompts, significantly accelerating development workflows.
