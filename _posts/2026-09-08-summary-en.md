## [Google DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome Atlas to Map Human DNA Variants](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/alphagenome-atlas/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google DeepMind has launched the AlphaGenome Atlas, an open-access database containing predictions for the effects of all 9 billion possible single nucleotide variants in the human genome on gene expression. This predictive map helps researchers understand how non-coding DNA and genetic variants influence gene expression, potentially accelerating the discovery of disease-causing mutations and therapeutic targets. While the atlas covers a massive scale of 9 billion potential mutations, some genomics experts note that the underlying AlphaGenome model offers incremental improvements over existing state-of-the-art models like Borzoi.

hackernews · utiiiD · Sep 8, 14:55 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49611251)

**Background**: Single nucleotide variants (SNVs) are the most common type of genetic variation among people, where a single nucleotide base in the DNA sequence is altered. Predicting how these variants affect gene expression—especially in non-coding regions of DNA—is crucial for understanding complex diseases, but has historically been difficult due to the vast search space and complex cellular mechanisms.
