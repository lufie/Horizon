## [Analyzing the Accuracy of AI Skeptic Ed Zitron's Predictions](https://danluu.com/zitron/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tech blogger Dan Luu has published a systematic evaluation of the predictions made by Ed Zitron, one of the tech industry's most prominent AI skeptics. The analysis objectively assesses how Zitron's warnings about the AI financial bubble and the collapse of major AI firms have panned out. In an AI landscape highly polarized between extreme hype and deep skepticism, this analysis provides a rare, objective look at the actual track record of AI critics. It helps the tech community move past rhetorical extremes and evaluate the real financial and technical viability of the AI boom. The evaluation focuses on the literal text of Zitron's predictions, such as his claim that OpenAI requires record funding and massive breakthroughs to survive. It highlights how critics often target the massive capital expenditures of hyperscalers and the potential financial engineering behind AI valuations.
