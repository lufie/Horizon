## [Release of Paged Out! Issue #9 Technical Zine](https://pagedout.institute/download/PagedOut_009.pdf) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Paged Out! team has released Issue #9 of their deeply technical, free e-zine, featuring highly focused, single-page articles on low-level programming, hacking, and computer science curiosities. It keeps the classic hacker "zine" culture alive, providing highly accessible yet deeply technical knowledge to systems programmers, security researchers, and retro-computing enthusiasts. This issue includes articles on topics ranging from C programming and subpixel rendering to computable tilings, maintaining the publication's signature highly-visual, single-page format.

hackernews · laurensr · Jul 27, 14:22 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49070138)

**Background**: Paged Out! is a free, non-profit community-driven magazine that focuses on low-level topics, security, and retro computing. It is inspired by classic underground hacker publications like Phrack and 2600 but features a modern, highly designed layout where each article is typically condensed into a single page.
