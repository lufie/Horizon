## [Measuring AI-Generated Writing on arXiv and the Limits of Detection](https://unslop.run/blog/measuring-ai-writing-on-arxiv) ⭐️ 8.0/10

An analysis of over 12,000 arXiv papers from 2021 to 2026 revealed a massive surge in AI-flagged writing since the release of ChatGPT, with up to 39% of all papers and 65% of computer science papers flagged as AI-written by January 2026. This study highlights the rapid integration of LLMs into academic writing, while simultaneously exposing the unreliability and high false-positive risks of AI detection tools in scientific publishing. Although the detector was tuned to minimize false positives, achieving a pre-ChatGPT baseline of 0.4%, mathematics papers remained largely unaffected at 0.7%, whereas computer science saw the most dramatic increase.

hackernews · dopamine_daddy · Jul 20, 16:36 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48981206)

**Background**: arXiv is a major open-access repository for scholarly articles in fields like physics, mathematics, and computer science. Since the launch of ChatGPT and other large language models, researchers have increasingly used these tools to assist in drafting papers, sparking intense debate over academic integrity and the validity of AI detection software.

**Discussion**: Users highlighted severe false-positive issues, noting that papers written long before the LLM era (e.g., in 2011 or 2015) were flagged with high machine-written scores, leading some to conclude that text-only AI detection is fundamentally unreliable.
