## [Private German Rocket Spectrum Achieves Historic Orbital Launch from Norway](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/isar-aerospace-second-launch-norway-andoya-spaceport-spectrum-rocket) ⭐️ 8.0/10

German aerospace startup Isar Aerospace has successfully launched its Spectrum rocket into orbit from the Andøya Spaceport in Norway. This milestone marks the first successful orbital launch of a privately developed European rocket from continental European soil. This launch represents a major leap forward for European space autonomy, reducing its reliance on American or Russian launch providers for small and medium satellites. It establishes a viable commercial orbital launch capability within Europe, fostering a competitive domestic space ecosystem. The Spectrum rocket is designed for small and medium-sized payloads, and this maiden flight successfully carried five small satellites and an in-flight technological experiment. The launch served as both a vehicle qualification mission and its first commercial payload delivery.

**Background**: Historically, European space missions have heavily relied on French Guiana (located in South America) for orbital launches, or on foreign partners like Russia and the US. Andøya Spaceport in Norway, which has hosted sub-orbital sounding rocket launches since 1962, was recently upgraded to support orbital launches to meet the growing demand for commercial satellite deployments.
