## [Terence Tao and Mathematicians Warn of AI Misalignment in Mathematics](https://mathandai.org/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Renowned mathematician Terence Tao and other prominent figures have raised concerns about a severe misalignment in how AI is being applied to mathematics, particularly through the generation of incomprehensible proofs. They argue that this trend, driven by major AI labs, threatens the traditional collaborative process of developing and sharing mathematical understanding. If AI systems focus solely on solving open problems with massive, unreadable proofs, it could undermine the core value of mathematics, which relies on human comprehension and conceptual breakthroughs. This shift could also disrupt how academic credit is assigned and how the mathematical community collaborates. The critique highlights that while AI can brute-force solutions to long-standing conjectures, these outputs often lack the explanatory power that helps humans learn. This creates a tension between "solving" a problem and "understanding" it, potentially turning mathematics into a black-box science.

**Background**: Traditionally, mathematics progress relies on peer-reviewed proofs that not only verify a statement's truth but also introduce new techniques and conceptual frameworks. With the rise of large language models and reinforcement learning, AI labs have increasingly targeted mathematical benchmarks, sometimes producing valid but highly complex, computer-verified proofs that human mathematicians struggle to decipher.

**Discussion**: Community members offer mixed perspectives, with some drawing parallels to historical anxieties, such as 19th-century criticisms of photography or 1990s fears that computers would ruin chess, which ultimately enriched those fields. Others note that while AI might not destroy human understanding, it disrupts traditional metrics for assigning academic credit and measuring mathematical contribution.
