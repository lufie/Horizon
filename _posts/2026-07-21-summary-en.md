## [Google Announces Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-6-flash-3-5-flash-lite-3-5-flash-cyber/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google has officially announced Gemini 3.6 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, and Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber, while also teasing that Gemini 4 development has begun. These models are designed to offer highly efficient, low-latency, and specialized capabilities for developers and enterprise tasks. This release highlights the industry trend toward smaller, highly specialized, and cost-effective models tailored for specific workflows like agentic search and cybersecurity. By offering tiered pricing and domain-specific fine-tuning, Google aims to capture high-volume enterprise workloads that do not require massive frontier models. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is optimized for subagent tasks and document parsing, priced at $0.30 per million input tokens, while Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is fine-tuned to detect and patch code vulnerabilities. Notably, Flash Cyber was evaluated on Google Chrome's production commit scanning pipeline to ensure the benchmark remained free of contamination.

**Background**: Google's Gemini family consists of different tiers, where 'Flash' models prioritize speed and cost-efficiency over the raw reasoning power of 'Pro' or 'Ultra' models. 'Lite' versions further reduce latency and cost for high-throughput tasks, while specialized variants leverage fine-tuning on domain-specific datasets to solve targeted industry problems like cybersecurity.
