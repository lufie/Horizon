## [Xiaomi Releases and Open-Sources MiMo-v2.6 Model Series](https://mimo.xiaomi.com/mimo-v2-6) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Xiaomi has released and open-sourced its MiMo-v2.6 model series, including the flagship MiMo-V2.6-Pro and the cost-effective MiMo-V2.6-Flash. These native multimodal models are designed for agent tasks such as programming, computer operation, and 3D scene creation, with their web experience, API, and Hugging Face models now accessible. The release highlights a growing trend of highly cost-effective and transparently trained Chinese open-weights models, offering developers affordable alternatives with unprecedented visibility into the training process. It also showcases Xiaomi's capability in executing massive reinforcement learning training runs at scale. The training utilized MixRL joint training for verifiable tasks and MOPD to merge capabilities for complex tasks, alongside the release of distilled Qwen models, 7000 diverse environments, and a complete RL framework. Xiaomi also shared a real-time training dashboard to ensure unprecedented transparency, and plans to release a Pro-UltraSpeed version that is up to 20 times faster.

hackernews · volf_ · Sep 21, 20:12 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49792730)

**Background**: Large language models increasingly rely on Reinforcement Learning (RL) to improve reasoning and agent capabilities, but these training runs are often resource-intensive and opaque. Open-weights models allow the community to run and fine-tune models locally, but true transparency regarding their training trajectories and methodologies remains rare in the industry.

**Discussion**: Users highly praised the transparency of the training process, particularly the real-time dashboard, and expressed excitement over the extreme cost-efficiency of Chinese models compared to US alternatives. Some participants also noted that China's superior energy infrastructure and grid buildout could give it a long-term advantage in the AI race by resolving data center power bottlenecks.
